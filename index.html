The Problem

What authors actually struggle with.

Five obstacles we see again and again, and how a personalized strategy addresses each.

The challenge

A great book nobody can find

Low visibility is rarely about quality. It is usually about how a book is positioned, listed and presented.

Our approach

We research how your readers search and rebuild your listing and visuals around it.

The challenge

Not knowing who the reader is

Without a defined audience, promotion becomes scattered and expensive.

Our approach

We map comparable titles, categories and reader interests before any campaign is planned.

The challenge

A launch with no coordination

Posts, ads, emails and reviews that do not support each other lose momentum.

Our approach

We build one launch timeline where every activity has a purpose.

The challenge

Weak positioning

If your book looks and sounds like everything else in its genre, readers scroll past.

Our approach

We sharpen the promise of your book and express it consistently.

The challenge

Limited reach beyond one market

Many authors never test the markets where their book could resonate.

Our approach

We plan market-aware promotion for the US and international readers.

How We Work
Our Philosophy

A book is not a product to be pushed. It is a conversation waiting for the right reader.

We start by understanding the book and the people it is for, then build marketing that respects both.

Our Process

Eight steps from first message to refined results.

1

Initial inquiry

You send a short note about your book and what you hope to achieve.

2

Book and author assessment

We review your book, genre, current listing, existing marketing and your goals, then identify what needs attention first.

3

Research and discovery

We study your comparable titles, reader keywords, categories and the markets that matter for your book.

4

Personalized strategy development

We shape a written strategy around your book's strengths, your audience and your budget.

5

Campaign planning

Timelines, deliverables and priorities are set so you know what happens and when.

6

Creative development

Graphics, mockups, copy and video assets are produced to a single consistent style.

7

Marketing execution

The campaign goes live across the agreed channels, with clear communication throughout.

8

Performance review and refinement

We look at what the data shows, report in plain language and adjust the plan for the next phase.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers to what authors ask us most.

Do you guarantee sales or bestseller rankings?

No. No honest marketer can. We guarantee careful research, a personalized plan, quality creative work and clear communication. Results depend on many factors, including the book itself and the market.

Which authors do you work with?

First-time, self-published, independent and established authors, as well as independent publishing houses, across fiction, nonfiction, romance, thriller, fantasy, children's, business and academic books.

Do you only work with authors in the United States?

The United States is our primary market, but we welcome authors from anywhere. We plan promotion around the markets that matter for your book.

How much does it cost?

Costs depend on your book and goals. Our Packages page outlines how packages are structured, and we can build a custom campaign for any budget. Tell us your goals and we will recommend a sensible starting point.

How do we communicate?

Primarily by email, so everything is documented. Consultations can be arranged by request.

Can you work on a single service, such as an Amazon listing?

Yes. You can start with one service, such as an audit or listing optimization, and expand later.
