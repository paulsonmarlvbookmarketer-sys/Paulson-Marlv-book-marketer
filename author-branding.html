Why branding

Readers follow authors, not only books.

A recognizable identity makes every book easier to discover and every promotion more effective. It is built from small, consistent choices across your cover style, website, social presence and communication.

  • Clear positioning in your genre
  • Visual consistency across channels
  • Stronger reader relationships
  • A foundation for future books
