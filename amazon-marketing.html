Home / Amazon Marketing

Amazon book marketing, built on research.

KDP optimization, listings, keywords, categories, advertising and audits.

Why it matters

Your Amazon page does the selling.

For many authors, the Amazon product page is where a reader decides. If the page is unclear, poorly keyworded or visually weak, even good promotion leaks readers at the final step.

We improve the page first, then support it with advertising and promotion. We do not promise rankings or sales, only careful, research-based improvement.

Keyword and category research tools in action.

What we do

Nine ways we improve your Amazon presence.

A

Amazon KDP marketing

Setup and positioning that help your book perform inside Amazon's ecosystem.

L

Listing optimization

Title, subtitle, description and A+ style content written for readers and search.

K

Keyword research

Identify the exact phrases your readers use when looking for books like yours.

C

Category research

Find categories where your book can be properly seen, not buried.

P

Product-page improvement

Cover presentation, mockups, formatting and copy that support the purchase decision.

A

A full review of your current Amazon presence with a prioritized fix list.

How it works

Research first. Then optimization. Then advertising.

  • Research keywords and categories
  • Rewrite and redesign listing elements
  • Plan and launch advertising, if appropriate
  • Review data and refine
