Who We Are

An international studio for book discoverability.

Paulson Marlv Book Marketing helps authors and publishers improve visibility, understand their readers and promote books with a clear, personalized strategy. We work remotely with clients around the world, with a primary focus on the United States.

We operate as a company, not a content factory: every engagement is planned individually and communicated clearly.

Paulson Marlv, Founder

Founder Story

It began with a simple frustration.

Too many good books go unseen. The writing is strong, the cover is professional, and yet readers never find them. The cause is rarely the book. It is usually missing strategy: unclear positioning, weak listings, scattered promotion, or no plan beyond launch day.

Paulson Marlv Book Marketing was founded to close that gap. The aim was to offer authors the kind of thoughtful, research-led marketing that large publishers take for granted, adapted to the realities of independent writing.

The business is young, and we say so plainly. What we bring is focus, craft and a commitment to treating each book as its own project.

Philosophy

Marketing that respects the book and the reader.

We believe the best book marketing begins with understanding. Who is this book for? What do those readers search for? Where do they spend time? Only then do we choose channels and craft messages.
