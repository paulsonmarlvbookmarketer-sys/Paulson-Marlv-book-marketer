Book launch marketing with a plan behind it.

From pre-launch preparation to post-launch visibility.

Launch Philosophy

A launch is a campaign, not a date.

The strongest launches begin weeks before publication and continue well after. We plan the whole arc so each activity supports the next.

We cannot guarantee outcomes, but we can ensure your launch is coordinated, well presented and aimed at the right readers.

Planning and scheduling the campaign timeline.

Campaign Timeline

Eight stages of a coordinated launch.

1

Pre-launch preparation

Positioning, listing, cover assets, audience research and review planning before anything goes public.

2

Launch campaign planning

A dated timeline, channel plan and clear responsibilities.

3

Promotional graphics

Announcement art, countdowns, quote cards and banners in one consistent style.

4

Social media promotion

Platform-appropriate content that builds curiosity ahead of launch.

5

Reader targeting

Defined reader groups, comparable titles and interest-based outreach.

6

Launch strategy

The key moves for launch week: messaging, timing and priorities.

7

Campaign execution

Posts, emails, outreach and ads delivered on schedule.

8

Post-launch visibility

Follow-up promotion and review so momentum does not vanish after week one.
